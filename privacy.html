Política de Privacidad de VibezMatch

Last updated: July 21, 2026

At VibezMatch, the privacy and safety of our neurodivergent community are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Data Accessed: Our application accesses Google user data through your Google Account, specifically your email address and public profile information (name and profile picture).

Data Usage: We use this information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To identify and authenticate you as a user within the app.
  • To create and personalize your user profile.
  • To ensure a secure login experience via Google Firebase Authentication.

Limited Use Disclosure: VibezMatch's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing: We do not share, sell, or use your Google user data for any purposes other than those described above. Your data is handled securely via Google Firebase.

En VibezMatch (desarrollado por AmySoftware), mantenemos una política estricta de tolerancia cero frente a cualquier tipo de abuso, explotación o vulneración de los derechos de menores de edad. Queda expresamente prohibido en nuestra plataforma:

1. Information We Collect

To provide the best user experience and ensure the functionality of our radar features, we collect:

  • Registration Data: Name, email address, and age.
  • Sensitive Profile Data: Information regarding neurodivergence and disability status (provided voluntarily).
  • Physical Attributes: Height and body type/build for radar filters.
  • Precise Location Data (GPS): Real-time location data for the "Radar" functionality.
  • Images and Camera Access: Access for identity verification via AI (ML Kit) and profile photos.

2. Use of Artificial Intelligence (Gemini AI)

VibezMatch utilizes the Gemini AI (Google Cloud) engine to analyze subtext and intent in chat communications, assisting users in navigating social interactions. Messages are processed using end-to-end encryption.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Transfer

We share necessary data with:

  • Google Firebase: Storage and authentication.
  • Google AdMob: For ad management.
  • Google Cloud / Gemini: For natural language processing.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Technical error reporting.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data under Consent (location and sensitive data), Performance of a Contract, and Legitimate Interest.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can request the total deletion of their account and all associated data at any time. To do so, please send an email to amyillustrator22@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Once the request is received, we will proceed to delete all your personal information, location history, and account credentials from our Firebase servers within a maximum period of 48 hours.

6. Data Security

We implement SSL/TLS encryption to protect your data against unauthorized access.
